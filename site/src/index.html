什么是 Coding VideoWhat Coding Video is

做动画视频大致有三条路。这个库只收第一条：画面全部由代码算出来。There are roughly three ways to make an animated video. This library covers only the first: every pixel comes from code.

代码逐帧Code, frame by frame

本库收录This library

AI 写 Canvas、WebGL、SVG 等渲染程序，无头浏览器按 t 逐帧截图，ffmpeg 编码。The AI writes a Canvas, WebGL or SVG renderer; a headless browser captures each frame at time t; ffmpeg encodes.

  • 可复现：同一份代码出同一支片Reproducible: same code, same film
  • 可改：换颜色、换文字、换节奏只改参数Editable: colors, words and timing are parameters
  • 没有素材版权问题No third-party assets to license

AI 生成画面AI-generated images

以后收（G 类）Later (class G)

画面由图像或视频模型生成，AI 助手写分镜和每个镜头的提示词。擅长写实人物，难保证跨镜头一致。An image or video model makes the pictures; the assistant writes the shot list and prompts. Strong at realistic people, weak at consistency across shots.

改编现成素材Existing footage

以后收（T 类）Later (class T)

在照片、录屏、口播原片、真实数据上加工。效果取决于手里的素材和授权。Work on top of photos, screen recordings, talking-head footage or real data. Depends on the material you have and its license.

一支样片怎么组成What a sample is made of

每支样片是一个文件夹里的两份代码，加一条共用的出片管线。横屏 16:9 和竖屏 9:16 用同一份代码，按画幅重新构图，不裁切。Each sample is two code files in one folder plus a shared render pipeline. Landscape 16:9 and portrait 9:16 come from the same code, recomposed for each frame shape rather than cropped.

  1. scene.js

    画面Picture

    一个 draw(ctx, t) 函数：给定时间 t，画出这一帧。不读随机数、不读时钟，所以结果唯一。One draw(ctx, t) function: given time t, draw that frame. No unseeded randomness, no clock, so the result is unique.

  2. sound.py

    声音Sound

    一个 score(m) 函数，用代码合成的电子音配乐：拨弦、FM 钟、方波、噪声扫频。不用 TTS、AI 作曲、曲库或录音。One score(m) function scoring the clip with synthesized sound: plucked strings, FM bells, square waves, noise sweeps. No TTS, AI music, libraries or recordings.

  3. render.py

    出片Render

    无头 Chromium 按 t = 帧号 ÷ 30 逐帧截图，交给 ffmpeg 编成 H.264，再和声音合成 MP4，顺手出静帧和缩略图。Headless Chromium captures each frame at t = frame ÷ 30, ffmpeg encodes H.264 and muxes the sound into an MP4, plus a still and a thumbnail.

提示词怎么写Writing the prompt

只写风格名，结果常常退化成通用扁平动画。真正起作用的是这 8 个要点，写得越具体越稳。A style name alone usually collapses into generic flat animation. These eight points do the work; the more specific, the steadier the result.

  1. 制作路径Production path写明“纯代码逐帧，不用生成模型和外部图片”。Say "pure code, frame by frame, no generators or external images".
  2. 3–5 个标志元素3–5 signature elements材质、限定调色板（写色值）、线条、留白、字体。Material, a limited palette with hex values, line quality, negative space, typeface.
  3. 运动语言Motion帧率、缓动、禁止项（不要弹跳、粒子、发光）。Frame rate, easing, and what to avoid (no bounce, particles, glow).
  4. 独有的转场A signature transition墨晕开、纸页折叠、网格扫过，放在高潮。Ink bloom, paper fold, grid wipe; place it at the climax.
  5. 镜头语言Camera卷轴横移、俯视、推拉、结尾拉远。Scroll-like pans, top-down views, push-ins, a closing pull-back.
  6. 声音Sound配乐乐器、音效跟着材质走、有没有旁白。Instruments, effects that match the material, narration or none.
  7. 参考作品References写清学构图、节奏、配色，不学角色和 Logo。Say what to learn (composition, rhythm, color) and what not to copy (characters, logos).
  8. 规格与验收Specs and checks时长、画幅、帧率；先出样片再出整片，每步抽帧自检。Length, frame shape, fps; sample before the full film, and check a contact sheet at every step.
