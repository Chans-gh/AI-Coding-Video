什么是 AI Coding VideoWhat AI Coding Video is

AI Coding Video 是让 AI 编程助手写程序来做视频：你用平常的话说想要什么片子，助手写出代码，代码一帧一帧画出画面、配好声音，最后存成 MP4。画面里的东西可以全部由代码画，也可以用你给的照片、录像、数据，或 AI 生成的图片和视频，混着用也行；不管素材从哪来，剪辑、排版、动效都由代码来做。下面用一支混着用的小片子走一遍。AI Coding Video means having an AI coding assistant write a program that makes the video. You say in plain words what you want; the assistant writes code; the code draws the picture frame by frame, adds the sound and saves an MP4. Everything in the frame can be drawn by code, or come from your own photos, footage and data, or from AI-generated images and clips, or any mix of them. Wherever the material comes from, the code does the editing, layout and motion. Here is one small mixed example, step by step.

  1. “做一支 15 秒的新品开场：用我拍的产品照片，背景让 AI 画一张，标题和评分做成动画。”"A 15-second product opener: use my product photo, have AI paint the background, and animate the title and the rating."

    你说想要什么Say what you want

    内容、风格、多长、横屏还是竖屏。手上有照片、录像、数据，一起交给它。What it's about, the look, how long, landscape or portrait. Hand over any photos, footage or data you have.

    • 代码画Code draws标题、评分、动效title, rating, motion
    • 实拍素材Real footage你拍的产品照片your product photo
    • AI 生成AI-made一张背景图one background

    备齐素材Gather the material

    从三处取，用哪几样都行。只用代码画的片子，这一步直接跳过。Take it from any of the three places, in any mix. A code-only film skips this step.

  3. 标题Title 照片Photo 背景Backdrop

    代码一层层叠成画面Code stacks the layers

    AI 写一段代码，说清每一刻每层放哪、怎么动，像叠透明胶片。The AI writes code saying where each layer sits and how it moves at every moment, like stacking clear film.

  4. t = 0.00 s

    出成视频Out comes the video

    每秒画 30 张，配上声音，存成 MP4，横屏竖屏各一版。同一份代码，每次出来都一样。30 pictures a second, plus sound, saved as MP4 in landscape and portrait. Same code, same film, every time.

第 3、4 步的小画面是你的浏览器此刻按代码现场画的。页面不放外来图片，所以「照片」和「AI 背景」用代码画的图形代替。The small pictures in steps 3 and 4 are being drawn by your browser right now, from code. This page uses no outside images, so the "photo" and the "AI background" are stand-ins drawn by code.

  • 可复现Reproducible同一份代码，每次跑出来的片子一帧不差。Same code, same film, frame for frame.
  • 可修改Editable换颜色、换文字、换节奏只是改几个参数。Colors, words and timing are a few parameters.
  • 素材来路清楚Clear provenance纯代码的样片没有素材；用素材的样片，每份素材的出处、许可或生成参数都记在清单里。Pure-code samples use no material; samples that do list every piece with its source, license or generation settings.

AI 用代码做视频的三类路线Three routes to making video with code

三类路线都由代码负责剪辑、排版和动效，区别在画面里的素材从哪来。点一类，下面的样片跟着切换。On every route the code does the editing, layout and motion; they differ in where the material in the frame comes from. Pick one and the samples below switch with it.

C 类 · {{N_C}} 种 · 按题材分Class C · {{N_C}} styles · by theme

T 类 · {{N_T}} 种 · 按内容类型分Class T · {{N_T}} styles · by content type

G 类 · {{N_G}} 种 · 按内容类型分Class G · {{N_G}} styles · by content type

先用生图、生视频、语音合成做出素材，再交给代码剪辑和加动效。适合写实人物和场景。任何一家服务都行。Generate images, clips and voice first, then let the code edit and animate them. Good for realistic people and scenes. Any provider works.

一支样片怎么组成What a sample is made of

每支样片是一个文件夹里的两份代码，加一条共用的出片管线；用素材的样片另有一个 assets/ 文件夹和一份素材清单 assets.json，记下每份素材的出处、许可或生成参数。横屏 16:9 和竖屏 9:16 用同一份代码，按画幅重新构图，不裁切。Each sample is two code files in one folder plus a shared render pipeline; samples with material also carry an assets/ folder and an assets.json list recording each piece's source, license or generation settings. Landscape 16:9 and portrait 9:16 come from the same code, recomposed for each frame shape rather than cropped.

  1. scene.js

    画面Picture

    一个 draw(ctx, t) 函数：给定时间 t，画出这一帧。不读随机数、不读时钟；视频素材按帧号取那一帧，所以结果唯一。One draw(ctx, t) function: given time t, draw that frame. No unseeded randomness, no clock; video material is read by frame number, so the result is unique.

  2. sound.py

    声音Sound

    一个 score(m) 函数。纯代码样片用代码合成的电子音：拨弦、FM 钟、方波、噪声扫频；用素材的样片照清单摆旁白、原声和公开曲库的配乐，说话时自动压低配乐。One score(m) function. Pure-code samples use synthesized sound: plucked strings, FM bells, square waves, noise sweeps. Samples with material place narration, original audio and openly licensed music from their asset list, ducking the music under speech.

  3. render.py

    出片Render

    无头 Chromium 按 t = 帧号 ÷ 30 逐帧截图，交给 ffmpeg 编成 H.264，再和声音合成 MP4，顺手出静帧和缩略图。Headless Chromium captures each frame at t = frame ÷ 30, ffmpeg encodes H.264 and muxes the sound into an MP4, plus a still and a thumbnail.

    const glow = smooth(.9, 2.0, t);           // 0.9–2.0 s：透出暖光 · warm light

提示词怎么写Writing the prompt

两种用法，提示词的重点不一样：风格演示要写清样式，也就是画面长什么样、怎么做；完整出片先说清内容和需求，每段的样式由助手分段时按段定，你看过再点头。The two ways of using it call for different prompts. A style demo needs the look spelled out: what the picture is and how it is made. A full film starts from the content and the brief; the assistant picks a look for each segment while breaking down the storyboard, and you approve it.

风格演示：写清样式的 8 个要点Style demo: eight points that pin down a look

只写风格名，结果常常退化成通用扁平动画。真正起作用的是这 8 个要点，写得越具体越稳。纯代码、真实素材、AI 素材三类都按这 8 点写；用素材的，另外写清素材从哪来、怎么处理。完整出片里每段的样式，也照这 8 点定。A style name alone usually collapses into generic flat animation. These eight points do the work; the more specific, the steadier the result. All three classes (pure code, real material, AI material) follow them; with material, also say where it comes from and how it is handled. In a full film, each segment's look is pinned down the same way.

  1. 制作路径Production path纯代码逐帧；或用哪些真实素材（录像、录屏、照片、数据）、出处和许可；或用哪个模型生图、生视频、合成旁白。代码负责剪辑、排版和动效。Pure code frame by frame; or which real material (footage, screen recordings, photos, data) with its source and license; or which model makes the images, clips and narration. Code does the editing, layout and motion.
  2. 3–5 个标志元素3–5 signature elements材质、限定调色板（写色值）、线条、留白、字体。用素材时写统一的色调、裁切和字幕样式；AI 素材先出一张定妆图当参考，前后才一致。Material, a limited palette with hex values, line quality, negative space, typeface. With material: one color grade, crop and caption style; for AI material, make a reference image first so every shot matches.
  3. 运动语言Motion帧率、缓动、禁止项（不要弹跳、粒子、发光）。素材片段的快慢、照片的推拉平移、数据怎么一步步长出来。Frame rate, easing, and what to avoid (no bounce, particles, glow). How fast clips run, how photos pan and push, how data builds up.
  4. 独有的转场A signature transition墨晕开、纸页折叠、网格扫过、漫画分格滑入，放在高潮。Ink bloom, paper fold, grid wipe, comic panels sliding in; place it at the climax.
  5. 镜头语言Camera推拉、横移、俯视、结尾拉远。真实素材写剪辑表（哪份素材的第几秒到第几秒、裁哪块）；AI 素材给每个镜头写首帧、动作和运镜。Push-ins, pans, top-down views, a closing pull-back. For real material, an edit list (which seconds of which file, cropped where); for AI material, each shot's first frame, action and camera move.
  6. 声音Sound配乐乐器、音效跟着材质走、有没有旁白。用素材的写清保不保留原声、旁白用哪个公开音色、配乐的许可。Instruments, effects that match the material, narration or none. With material: keep the original sound or not, which stock voice narrates, the music's license.
  7. 参考作品References写清学构图、节奏、配色，不学角色和 Logo。Say what to learn (composition, rhythm, color) and what not to copy (characters, logos).
  8. 规格与验收Specs and checks时长、画幅、帧率；先出样片再出整片，每步抽帧自检。用素材的先列素材清单（出处和许可，或模型、参数和提示词），出片前去掉元数据。Length, frame shape, fps; sample before the full film, and check a contact sheet at every step. With material, list every piece first (source and license, or model, settings and prompt) and strip metadata before export.

完整出片：先说清这 8 样Full film: eight things to say first

不用一开始就选风格。说清给谁看、讲什么，助手先分段（每段讲什么、几秒、用什么样式，一段可以混用几种）、把段拆成分镜（每个画面画什么、几秒、配哪句旁白），再写逐字稿，再定全片统一的色板、字体、字幕、转场、配乐和旁白音色。它补的数据和事实都标「待你确认」，你点头才进片子。No need to choose a style up front. Say who it is for and what it says; the assistant first splits the film into segments (what each says, how long, which looks, several mixed in one segment if needed), breaks segments into shots (what each picture shows, how long, which line it goes with) and writes a script, then sets what stays the same across the film: palette, type, captions, transitions, music and narration voice. Any data or facts it adds are marked for your confirmation and go in only when you approve.

  1. 给谁看Who it is for受众是谁、在哪儿看：刚开始做饭的大学生、新员工培训、发在视频号。The audience and where they watch: students who just started cooking, new-hire training, a social video channel.
  2. 看完得到什么What they take away一句话：看完知道什么、会做什么、记住什么。One sentence: what they know, can do or remember afterwards.
  3. 一句话主线The one-line story整支片子围着哪句话转，比如「水开了不等于够热」。The line the whole film turns on, such as "boiling isn't the same as hot enough".
  4. 时长和画幅Length and frame几分钟；横版 16:9（默认）或竖版 9:16，只出一版。How many minutes; landscape 16:9 (the default) or portrait 9:16, one version only.
  5. 语言和旁白Language and narration什么语言，要不要旁白。旁白用语音合成的公开音色，也可以用本地 Kokoro；要用自己的声音，请自带克隆声音的第三方工具。Which language, narrated or not. Narration uses a stock voice from a speech service, or local Kokoro; to use your own voice, bring your own voice-cloning tool.
  6. 内容素材Your content你手上的稿子、要点、数据、链接，贴给它整理；只有一个主题的，它从主题往外扩展。内容多、要查证的，先用 AI-content-planner 做策划。Drafts, notes, data or links you already have, for it to organize; with only a topic, it expands from there. For heavy, fact-checked content, plan it first with AI-content-planner.
  7. 必须有和不要Musts and don'ts一定要出现的品牌色、产品、口播句；不想要的画风、说法、音乐。Brand colors, products or lines that must appear; looks, phrasing or music to avoid.
  8. 怎么确认How you sign off先给段落表、分镜表和逐字稿，确认后先做 1–2 段带旁白的典型样片，满意了再出整片。Segment table, shot lists and script first; after sign-off, one or two narrated typical segments; the whole film once you are happy.

什么是 SkillWhat a Skill is

Skill（技能包）是写给 AI 编程助手的一份说明书：一个文件夹，核心是一份 SKILL.md，旁边放着它要用的资料和脚本。助手平时只记得它的一句简介；你的话题碰到做视频时，它才把整份说明和用得上的词典条目读进来，照着做。A Skill is a manual written for an AI coding assistant: a folder built around one SKILL.md, with the reference material and scripts it needs. The assistant normally keeps only its one-line summary in mind; when your request touches on making a video, it reads the full manual and the dictionary entries it needs, and follows them.

所以你不用记风格名和参数，也不用把提示词一段段贴给它。说「给一支讲水循环的科普片选个风格」，它会去查词典、给你候选、写代码、出样片。So you don't need to remember style names or parameters, or paste prompts in. Say "pick a style for an explainer on the water cycle" and it looks up the dictionary, offers candidates, writes the code and renders a sample.

完整出片：做一支几分钟的片子Full film: a video several minutes long

  1. 定内容Settle the content

    有策划稿的照着接；没有的，助手整理或扩展你给的内容，补的事实标「待你确认」。With a plan from AI-content-planner, it follows it; without one, it organizes or expands what you give, marking any facts it adds for your confirmation.

  2. 分段、分镜与样式Segments, shots and looks

    分段、每段讲什么、几秒、用什么样式，一段可以混用几种风格、技术和素材；段里再拆成分镜：每个画面画什么、几秒、配哪句旁白；同时定全片统一。Segments, what each says, how long, which looks, mixing styles, techniques and material within a segment; each segment broken into shots: what each picture shows, how long, which line; plus what stays the same across the film.

  3. 逐字稿Script

    每段念什么，画面上每个数字和事实从哪来。What each segment says aloud, and where every number and fact on screen comes from.

  4. 你确认Your sign-off

    一次看段落表、每段的分镜表、全片统一、逐字稿和待确认的事实；两张表由检查脚本打印，不手抄。Segment table, each segment's shot list, film-wide settings, script and facts to confirm, all at once; a check script prints both tables.

  5. 典型样片Typical segment

    先做 1–2 段最难或最能代表全片的，带旁白和配乐，用整片的画幅。One or two of the hardest or most representative segments first, with narration and music, in the final frame shape.

  6. 渲染整片The whole film

    配完全部旁白、定好段长，分段渲染、单独检查，再接成整片、统一混音。All narration done and segment lengths set, then render and check segment by segment, join them and mix the sound once.

默认出一版：横版 16:9，要竖版就只出竖版，不另剪短片。做法和格式见 Skill 里的 film.md；下面「真实应用」是完整出片的示范。One version by default: landscape 16:9, or portrait only if you need it, with no extra cut-downs. The method and formats are in the Skill's film.md; "In real work" below shows full films made with it.

AI-Coding-Video 真实应用AI Coding Video in real work

样片演示一种风格、一个技术；这里是把它们拼成一条能交付的完整视频。每支都用到 C 代码画、T 真实素材、G AI 生成三类，各有一个主角类。点卡片开始播放，右边的说明跟着进度切到当前这一段：用了哪几类、哪些技术（点了跳到对应样片）、怎么编排的；进度条按段着色，颜色是那段的主角类。Each sample shows one style or one technique; here they are combined into complete, deliverable videos. Every video uses all three kinds (C code-drawn, T real material, G AI-generated), with one kind in the lead. Click a card to play; the notes on the right follow the playhead to the current part: which kinds and techniques it uses (each links to its sample) and how it was put together. The progress bar is split by part and colored by each part's lead kind. The videos and notes are in Chinese.
